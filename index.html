Who We Are

Opening Doors to Education and Opportunity

House JhwhEl is focused on helping people prepare for educational, professional, and personal opportunities, particularly those connected to Germany.

We believe that the right information, proper preparation, and reliable support can make an unfamiliar journey easier to navigate. Our approach is centred on dignity, practical guidance, and helping people make informed decisions.

Travel With Better Preparation

Support Before Departure and After Arrival

Preparing to travel can involve more than booking a flight. We aim to help individuals understand the process and identify the support they may need along the way.

Your Journey Matters

From travel preparation to settling into a new environment.

Discuss Your Travel Plans
01

Document Preparation

Understand relevant travel documentation and arrangements, including journeys to Abuja when in-person document collection is required.

02

Flights and Airline Coordination

Obtain guidance about flight planning, airline enquiries, check-in procedures, and available assistance before departure.

03

Airport Assistance

Plan ahead for airport procedures and enquire about available support in Nigeria and Germany.

Prepare for arrival, accommodation arrangements, local orientation, and the first steps towards settling into Germany.

Travel assistance, airport reception, hotel arrangements, airline coordination, and local support are subject to confirmation and availability. Flight bookings, visas, and travel documents remain subject to the relevant providers and authorities.
